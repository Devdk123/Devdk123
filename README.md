## About

I build full-stack applications end-to-end — from database schema to REST API layer to user-facing interface. My primary stack is MERN, and I'm actively expanding into TypeScript, Next.js, and relational databases as the systems I build grow in complexity.

I participate in hackathons to sharpen both technical and product-thinking instincts. Under time pressure, you make real architectural decisions fast — and learn exactly what matters in a codebase. Projects like **CivicFix** and **NexZen** were built under those conditions and have continued to evolve since.

Outside of shipping features, I'm building a consistent DSA practice and studying backend patterns, database design, and the foundations of intelligent systems — the kind of engineering depth that compounds over time.

## Hackathons & Building

Hackathons are where I test how clearly I can think and how fast I can execute. The constraint isn't the problem — it's the point. It forces you to scope tightly, make architecture decisions quickly, and ship something that actually works by the deadline.

**CivicFix** and **NexZen** both originated in hackathon environments. What I take away from each one goes beyond the project itself: sharper judgment on what makes a good MVP, faster decision-making on stack choices, and a clearer sense of what it takes to ship under pressure.
